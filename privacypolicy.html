Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 · Applies to Flux and all its features

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Flux, an independent browser-game and social platform operated by nxtcoreee3 (“Flux”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit or use the Flux website and its features.

By using Flux, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use Flux. This policy applies to Flux services and pages that link to it, but not to third-party websites, games, or services that Flux does not control.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Flux may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: username, display name, email address, authentication identifiers, profile photo, biography, and account settings.
  • Community information: profiles, follows, followers, reactions, reviews, reports, chat messages, direct messages, and other content you choose to submit.
  • Gameplay and preference information: favourites, recently played games, game-server selections, ratings, unlocked game records, and gameplay-related activity.
  • Technical information: browser type, device and operating-system information, approximate network information, IP address, timestamps, language, and referring page where made available by your browser or service providers.
  • Security and moderation information: reports, account restrictions, abuse-prevention signals, moderation decisions, and records needed to protect Flux and its users.

We do not ask you to submit sensitive personal information. Please do not post passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information in public profiles, chats, reviews, or messages.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide, maintain, and personalize Flux features;
  • Authenticate users and maintain account sessions;
  • Save favourites, preferences, game activity, and other requested settings;
  • Operate profiles, chat, messaging, reviews, and social features;
  • Monitor performance, diagnose errors, and improve reliability;
  • Detect fraud, abuse, harmful content, or violations of Flux rules;
  • Respond to requests, reports, and support or moderation matters; and
  • Comply with applicable law and protect the rights, safety, and security of Flux, its users, and third parties.

4. Authentication and Service Providers

Flux uses third-party infrastructure, including Firebase and Google services, for authentication, database storage, analytics, and related platform functions. Depending on the feature and your account status, these providers may process identifiers, email address, IP address, device information, session data, and activity needed to provide the service.

Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. You can review Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase’s privacy information.

5. Cookies, Local Storage, and Similar Technologies

Flux uses cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar browser technologies for essential functions and preferences, including:

  • Keeping you signed in and maintaining security state;
  • Remembering cookie consent, policy acceptance, theme, navigation, and game-server preferences;
  • Saving local favourites, recently played games, and locally stored game progress; and
  • Supporting performance, analytics, moderation, and platform functionality where enabled.

Local data is stored in your browser and may be lost if you clear site data, use private browsing, switch devices, or apply browser restrictions. Some third-party games may use their own cookies or storage when opened or embedded; those practices are controlled by the relevant game or provider.

6. Analytics and Advertising

Flux may use analytics and advertising services to understand general site usage, maintain the service, and support its operation. These services may process technical identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, page views, and interaction data according to their own policies and applicable consent settings.

Advertising content may be delivered by Flux or third-party advertising providers. We do not sell your personal information. Where a third-party provider uses cookies or interest-based advertising, that provider is responsible for its own disclosures, controls, and consent requirements.

7. Games and External Providers

Flux is a directory and access point for browser games. Some games are hosted by Flux or developers, while others are provided through external catalogs such as ZapGames and Famobi.

When you select External Cloud Gaming, Flux may load provider metadata, thumbnails, and game URLs, and may open a game in Flux’s iframe player. Your browser may connect directly to the provider or game host. The provider may receive your IP address, browser and device information, referring page, cookies, and activity according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Flux does not control external providers’ content, advertising, tracking, availability, security, data retention, or privacy practices. Review the relevant provider’s policies before using external games:

  • ZapGames Privacy Policy
  • Famobi website and terms

8. Community Content and Moderation

Content you submit to public or private Flux features may be stored and processed to provide those features. Public profiles, reviews, and other public submissions may be visible to other users. Private messages are intended for their participants, but authorized administrators may access or review them when reasonably necessary for safety, moderation, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or enforcement of Flux rules.

We may remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts, or preserve relevant records when necessary to protect the community or enforce our rules.

9. How We Share Information

Flux may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: with infrastructure, authentication, database, analytics, security, and advertising providers that help operate Flux;
  • External game providers: when you choose to access their catalogs or games;
  • Safety and legal matters: when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, enforce rules, respond to lawful requests, or protect rights, safety, and security; or
  • Business changes: if Flux or its assets are reorganized, transferred, or combined with another service, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their independent marketing purposes outside the services described above.

10. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested feature, maintain account and security records, resolve disputes, enforce rules, comply with legal obligations, or protect Flux and its users. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No website, database, or transmission method is completely secure, and Flux cannot guarantee absolute security. External games and provider content are used at your own risk.

11. Children’s Privacy

Flux is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Flux, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

12. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of certain personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

  • You can update certain profile information through Flux settings.
  • You can clear local cookies and storage through your browser settings.
  • You can stop using external providers or embedded games at any time.
  • You can request account or data deletion by contacting us through the Flux GitHub repository.

We may need to verify your request before taking action. Some information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

13. International Processing

Flux and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers in accordance with applicable law.

14. Automated Service Monitoring

Flux may perform automated checks of its pages, services, and configured domains to display availability information. These checks are intended to measure service reachability and performance and are separate from the privacy practices of the domain owners and external providers being checked.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Flux features, providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the latest version. Continued use of Flux after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

16. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about this policy, contact the Flux operator through the Flux GitHub repository. Please include enough information for us to understand and verify your request, but do not include passwords or other sensitive credentials.
